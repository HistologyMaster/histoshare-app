TITLE: The histology of the paraphysis of Amblystoma
ACCESS: Wiley passage (stored excerpt) only
STORED EXCERPT: The paraphysis of adult Amblystoma is made up of low columnar ependymal epithelit m which forms the paraphyseal tubules which end blindly and which communicate with one another by a common mouth with the third ventricle. Between the paraphyseal tubules venous sinusoids anastomose freely with one another forming a complicated rete. The sinusoids are made up entirely of endothelium. The blood supply to the paraphysis is entirely venous.

Mitochondria were found in great abundance in the paraphysis of one female just previous to laying. Other specimens showed very few present.
