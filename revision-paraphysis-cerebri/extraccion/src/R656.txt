The paraphysis, which has probably a glandular function in some of the lower vertebrates, is not represented in the brain of the placental mammal.

The velum transversum and dorsal sac give rise to the choroid plexuses of the lateral and third ventricles. The arrangement of the choroid plexus of the third ventricle, the relationships of the dorsal sac and the histological appearances of these structures are described.
