The paraphysis cerebri is a glandular organ developed in amphibians and nonavian reptiles at the level of the 3rd ventricle (Hinton etal. [1990](#joa12613-bib-0027)). Variations in the size of the paraphysis cerebri are evident among the climberjumper *Hypsiboas pulchellus* (Fig.[6](#joa12613-fig-0006)D) and the climberwalkers *P.sauvagii* and *P.boliviana*, being bigger in the latter two species (Fig.[6](#joa12613-fig-0006)E,F), and less developed and cordonlike in the hopperwalker *R. fernandezae* (Fig.[6](#joa12613-fig-0006)C).
